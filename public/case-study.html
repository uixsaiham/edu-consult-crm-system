04 / Problem statement & business goals
04 / PROBLEM STATEMENT & BUSINESS GOALS

Disconnected work.
A student journey without clarity.

When information lives everywhere, progress is hard to see. The opportunity: bring every team, update, and next step together.

THE PROBLEM

Recruitment teams rely on disconnected spreadsheets, messages, and manual updates to track student applications.

Student details, documents, follow-ups, and application progress sit across separate tools. Counsellors and agents have to piece together the latest information before they can take the next step.

Why it matters

When ownership and next steps are unclear, follow-ups slip, deadlines become harder to manage, and students wait longer for answers. Managers struggle to see where applications are blocked or where teams need support.

THE PRODUCT NEEDS TO ACHIEVE

One shared workspace that makes every student’s status, owner, and next action clear.

User pain points

  1. 01
    No reliable view of progress

    Teams search across spreadsheets and conversations to find the latest application status.

  2. 02
    Follow-ups depend on memory

    Scattered reminders make it easy to miss a document request, deadline, or student update.

  3. 03
    Handoffs lose context

    Counsellors, agents, and finance teams repeat checks because history and ownership are unclear.

Business objectives

  1. 01
    Centralize the student journey

    Connect enquiries, applications, documents, and communication in one shared record.

  2. 02
    Reduce delays and manual work

    Surface urgent actions, assign ownership, and help teams resolve blockers sooner.

  3. 03
    Make performance visible

    Give managers a clear view of the pipeline, conversion, and branch and team performance.

05 / Problem definition & research
05 / PROBLEM DEFINITION & RESEARCH
User questions & design direction

User questions
driving product clarity.

Secondary research and competitor review shaped these questions. The themes below are working hypotheses that guide the CRM’s design direction.

Who is responsible for the next student update?

Ownership / Design focus

Keep responsibility visible so every handoff has a clear next owner.

What slows down an application?

Blockers / Design focus

Surface missing documents, pending responses, and other obstacles alongside progress.

What helps teams decide what to do next?

Clarity / Design focus

Connect status, deadlines, and next actions in one shared view.

Where can teams find the latest student details?

Context / Design focus

Keep student history, documents, and notes together in a consistent record.

Which follow-ups need attention today?

Priority / Design focus

Bring approaching deadlines and outstanding follow-ups into daily focus.

How can managers see where support is needed?

Visibility / Design focus

Make workloads and blocked applications easier to review across the team.

Research basis: secondary research & competitor review. Provisional themes; supporting source examples and user validation remain pending. No survey percentages are claimed.
